A broader healthy-hair menu, not a one-size-fits-all service.

Healthy Hair & Treatments at Pazzion Stylez include protein treatments, deep conditioning, scalp massages, blowouts, roller sets, silk press services, and natural-hair care. The appropriate appointment depends on the service you want, your current hair needs, the complexity of the work, and the professional you select.

Pazzion Stylez is founded and led by LaToya Terry, “The Hair Doctor,” a Master Cosmetologist and Cosmetology Instructor with 20+ years in cosmetology. Salon and product descriptions are limited to cosmetic and wellness care and are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease.

Before you book

No deposit is required for any service, including consultations. New clients: A $25 consultation is required before the first Pazzion Stylez service and is applied toward the first completed service total.

Returning clients: Book directly with your preferred stylist unless an additional consultation is requested or needed.

SERVICE GUIDE

Build your appointment around the service you actually need.

Treatments and conditioning

Protein treatments and deep conditioning are listed within the Pazzion Stylez Healthy Hair & Treatments category. When deciding what to book, consider your recent services and the result you are trying to achieve. If you are new to Pazzion Stylez or uncertain which treatment listing applies, start with the consultation instead of guessing. You can also bring questions about your current routine and maintenance goals.

Natural-hair styling options

Natural-hair care on the menu sits alongside blowouts, roller sets, and silk press services. Those are different appointment types and may not be interchangeable. Choose your stylist first, then use that professional’s Square service list to confirm which natural-hair or styling options are currently available.

Scalp and routine care

The service menu also references scalp massages, while Pazzion Stylez products include scalp and moisture-care items. Keep cosmetic salon care separate from medical care: concerns that may require diagnosis or treatment belong with a qualified healthcare professional. For routine salon needs, use the service chooser and contact form to discuss the appropriate appointment.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Healthy-hair service questions

What services are included in the Healthy Hair category?

Pazzion Stylez lists protein treatments, deep conditioning, scalp massages, blowouts, roller sets, silk press services, and natural-hair care within the broader Healthy Hair & Treatments menu.

Does the website promise a specific treatment result?

No. Service descriptions explain cosmetic salon options without promising medical or guaranteed outcomes. Individual hair needs and the selected service affect what is appropriate.

How do I know which stylist offers the service I want?

Use the website booking chooser. After you select LaToya or Kottia and your client status, Square shows the services and appointment availability assigned to that professional.
