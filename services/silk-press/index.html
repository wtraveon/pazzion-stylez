Silk press within the Pazzion Stylez healthy-hair menu.

A silk press is a smooth, straightened finish created on natural hair with heat styling rather than a permanent chemical straightener. At Pazzion Stylez, silk press is listed within the broader Healthy Hair & Treatments menu alongside deep conditioning, scalp care, blowouts, roller sets, and natural-hair services.

For a silk press appointment in Wilson, North Carolina, choose the stylist first. Square then shows the services and appointment times currently assigned to that professional, so you can confirm current availability before leaving the Pazzion Stylez website.

Before you book

No deposit is required for any service, including consultations. New clients: A $25 consultation is required before the first Pazzion Stylez service and is applied toward the first completed service total.

Returning clients: Book directly with your preferred stylist unless an additional consultation is requested or needed.

SERVICE GUIDE

Know what to confirm before booking a silk press.

Start with your current hair and recent service history

A styling decision can be affected by what has recently been done to the hair. When you are new to Pazzion Stylez, use the required consultation to discuss the service you want and any relevant color, chemical, or styling history. Returning clients should contact the salon if they are unsure which current booking listing applies. Ask about preparation or maintenance whenever those details are unclear before booking.

Separate silk press from other healthy-hair appointments

Silk press, blowouts, roller sets, deep conditioning, and natural-hair care are all referenced by Pazzion Stylez, but they are not the same service. Book the specific option available with your chosen stylist rather than selecting another listing simply because the appointment duration or name appears similar.

Use realistic maintenance expectations

A silk press changes the finished style; it does not permanently change the natural texture. Moisture, activity, weather, and at-home handling can affect how long a smooth finish lasts. Pazzion Stylez does not make a fixed longevity promise on this page because results vary by hair and circumstances.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Silk-press questions

Is a silk press a chemical relaxer?

No. A silk press is a heat-styled smooth finish on natural hair, while relaxers are chemical services. Pazzion Stylez lists chemical services separately from its Healthy Hair & Treatments category.

Do new Pazzion Stylez clients book the silk press first?

No deposit is required for consultations or any other service. New clients begin with the required $25 consultation before the first Pazzion Stylez service. The consultation fee is applied toward the first completed service total.

Can I see which stylist currently offers silk press appointments?

Yes. Choose LaToya or Kottia in the website booking chooser. The connected Square route shows the services and appointment times currently available for that selected professional.
