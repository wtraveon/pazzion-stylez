HOW INFORMATION IS HANDLED

Privacy, explained clearly.

Use this notice to understand the general information practices associated with the website. It is not a promise that every third-party service follows the same practices.

Information you choose to provide

When you contact us or request salon information, you may provide details such as your name, email address, phone number, preferred stylist, and the message you send. Please do not submit payment-card details, passwords, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information through website forms.

How information may be used

Information you submit may be used to respond to questions, discuss services or products, coordinate appointment inquiries, provide information you request, protect forms from spam or misuse, and maintain or improve the website and services.

Hosting, security, and technical data

Website hosting, security, spam-prevention, and technical services may process information such as browser details, IP address, and request data to deliver and protect the site. The website forms use Cloudflare Turnstile for spam verification, which may process your IP address and verification details. When a form submission succeeds, the information is sent to the salon’s configured notification email through Cloudflare’s email service. These providers have their own terms and privacy practices. Internet transmission cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Sharing and retention

We do not intend to sell personal information submitted through this website. Information may be shared with service providers needed to operate the site or respond to you, or when disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety. Inquiries are retained only as reasonably needed for business, legal, and security purposes.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children without appropriate parent or guardian involvement.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about information you have submitted or request a correction or deletion where applicable. We may need to retain certain information for legitimate business, legal, or security reasons.

Third-party links and services

Links to social media, booking tools, email services, and other third-party websites are provided for convenience. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Review those policies before sharing information with them.

Photos and video

Photo or video permission is a separate choice. Please review the Photo & Video Release page to understand the consent options before authorizing public use of images.
