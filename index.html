FAQ

Before you book.

Need something not covered here? Call Pazzion Stylez Salon / Appointments / Consultations at 252-468-1243.

Do new clients need a consultation?

No deposit is required for any service or consultation. Clients who are new to Pazzion Stylez must complete a $25 consultation before their first service. The $25 consultation fee is applied toward the first service total. Returning Pazzion Stylez clients do not need another new-client consultation unless one is specifically requested or needed.

Can I choose my stylist?

Yes. Choose LaToya Terry or Kottia Cotton for online booking. Contact JahNiqua Speight directly to confirm her services and availability.

Does Kottia offer every service shown on the Pazzion Stylez website?

Not necessarily. The website shows the overall Pazzion Stylez service menu. When you choose Kottia, Square displays only the services currently available for booking with her.

Can I switch stylists?

Yes. Existing Pazzion Stylez clients may book either stylist and do not need another new-client consultation solely because they change stylists.

Why don’t I see a certain service after choosing my stylist?

Services vary by stylist. If a service does not appear after you choose your stylist, it is not currently available for online booking with that professional. Contact Pazzion Stylez if you need help selecting the correct service.

How long should I expect my appointment to take?

Appointments can range from approximately 30 minutes to 12 hours depending on the service, hair length, density, preparation, and service complexity.

Can I buy products without an appointment?

Yes. Shop available Pazzion Stylez products online through our secure Square store. Pickup and shipping options are shown during checkout.
